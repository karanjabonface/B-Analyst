Services We Offer

At Starehe Resort, we strive to make your stay comfortable and memorable. Our services may vary slightly by location, but generally include:

  • Restaurant & Bar: Enjoy delicious local and international cuisine, and relax with a drink at our bar.
  • Swimming Pool: Take a refreshing dip in our pool (available at select locations).
  • Free Wi-Fi: Stay connected with complimentary internet access in rooms and public areas.
  • Spa & Wellness: Rejuvenate with relaxing treatments (available at select premium locations).
  • Conference Facilities: Host your meetings and events with us (available at select locations).
  • Laundry Service: Convenient laundry and dry-cleaning services.
  • Tour & Excursion Desk: Let us help you plan local activities, safaris, and excursions.

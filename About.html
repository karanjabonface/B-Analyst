About Starehe Resort

Founded with a passion for hospitality and a love for Kenya's natural beauty, Starehe Resort aims to provide unforgettable vacation experiences. Our journey began with a single location, driven by the desire to offer guests a place of peace ('Starehe' in Swahili) combined with comfort and adventure.

Our mission is to deliver exceptional service, comfortable accommodations, and authentic experiences that showcase the best of each region we operate in. We are committed to sustainable tourism practices and supporting local communities.

Our Branches

We are proud to have branches in some of Kenya's most popular vacation destinations:

  • Maasai Mara: Experience the wild heart of Kenya.
  • Diani Beach: Relax on pristine white sandy beaches.
  • Naivasha: Enjoy the serene lake and wildlife.
  • Malindi: Explore coastal history and marine life.
  • Nanyuki: Discover the gateway to Mount Kenya.
  • Nairobi: A convenient city escape or starting point.
